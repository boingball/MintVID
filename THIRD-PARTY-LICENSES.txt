Third-party components used by mrplay
-------------------------------------

libmpeg2 (VideoLAN)
  Licence: GPL-2.0-or-later
  Source:  player/vendor/libmpeg2/
  Release notice: LICENSES/GPL-2.0.txt and LICENSES/libmpeg2-README.txt
  MintVID adds libmpeg2/motion_comp_swar.c (four pixels per 32-bit word)
  and sparse-block shortcuts in libmpeg2/idct.c, both under the same
  GPL-2.0-or-later terms. The four-way half-pel average uses the byte-split
  technique of Henryk Richter's MacrosInterpol68k.m from RiVA 0.54
  (GPL-2.0-or-later); the file header says what was changed.

Rockbox/a52dec-derived fixed-point AC-3 core
  Licence: GPL-2.0-or-later
  Source:  player/vendor/liba52/
  The GPL text supplied with the release also applies to this component.
  Copyright/licence notices remain in the corresponding source files.

libdv (Quasar DV Codec) - decode-only subset
  Licence: LGPL-2.1-or-later
  Source:  player/vendor/libdv/
  Release notice: player/vendor/libdv/COPYING.LGPL-2.1
  Vendored for DV (IEC 61834/SMPTE 314M) video decode - core/mr_dv.c is the
  MintVID-side codec plugin, covering both DV/PAL (4:2:0) and DV/NTSC or
  PAL/SMPTE 314M DVCPRO (4:1:1). Only the decode-only files are carried (no
  encoder, no RGB colour-space output, no popt CLI helper - see each file's
  own "MintVID adaptation" comment for what changed and why: dropped
  pthread locking, a corrected endian-detection shim in
  player/vendor/libdv/mr_dv_endian.h, and a pitches-array type fix). YUY2.c
  is kept (unlike RGB): libdv has no planar 4:1:1 renderer, only a packed-
  YUY2 one, and core/mr_dv.c downsamples that itself. Upstream:
  http://libdv.sourceforge.net/
